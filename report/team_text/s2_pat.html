2.2 Q2. What patterns did we discover during EDA?

Our exploratory analysis revealed several interesting characteristics of the dataset:

  • There were no missing values.
  • Around 94% of GPS steps were exactly one second apart.
  • The training dataset contained 1,216 time gaps longer than five seconds.
  • Approximately 92% of roads had a reverse-direction twin.
  • The road network was divided into many connected components, although there were no isolated nodes.
  • Out of 90 routes, 11 were non-contiguous.
  • The median GPS noise was approximately 5.7 metres from the actual road.

These observations helped us understand why identifying the correct road was not always straightforward.
