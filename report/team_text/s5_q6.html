An important observation was that all 10 student-test trips improved after applying the HMM. The HMM and classifier also proved to be complementary. When we used the HMM alone with Gaussian emissions, it achieved only 88.71% accuracy, which was lower than the classifier's performance. However, when we combined the HMM with any of the seven classifiers, accuracy improved by approximately 2.4 to 8.9 percentage points.

Where did the HMM help the least? There were two notable cases. Trip 00000007: accuracy improved by only 0.4 percentage points; this trip already had an accuracy of 94.8%, so there was relatively little room for improvement. Trip 00000077: accuracy was 81.9%; in this case, both possible interpretations of the ambiguous road sections formed connected routes. Since both were valid from a connectivity perspective, the transition model could not easily distinguish between them.
