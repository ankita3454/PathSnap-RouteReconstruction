Distance and direction were the most important feature groups, together contributing 71.8% of the feature importance (Fig 13).

Example of how features help. Consider row 200 in our notebook. Two candidate roads were both approximately 6.76 metres away from the GPS point. Based on distance alone, both roads appeared equally likely. However, their directional alignment was very different: the correct road had a 12° difference and the incorrect road a 168° difference. This shows why direction features are particularly useful for distinguishing between roads travelling in opposite directions.
