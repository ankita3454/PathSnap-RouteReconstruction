Training and evaluation procedure

To compare different machine learning models fairly, we followed a consistent training and evaluation procedure. We used 5-fold GroupKFold cross-validation across the 78 training trips. The grouping was done by trip rather than individual GPS points. This ensured that points belonging to the same trip could not appear in both training and validation folds, preventing data leakage. Each model was then retrained using the complete training dataset and evaluated once on the student-test dataset. We also fixed the random seed to 42 to ensure reproducibility.
